Protection & privacy

You decide what stays private.

One private page or an entire notebook: choose what needs a password. Associated attachments are protected too.

One password for many pages

The notebook's vault protects selected pages and their attachments with one passphrase. All other pages stay open.

A password of its own per page

Single pages get a password of their own, independent of the vault. Every page can have a different one – attachments included.

A sealed notebook

Everything is encrypted, including the names of pages and attachments on disk. The passphrase is asked for when you open it.

FAQ

Good to know

Do I have to install anything?

No. Mumbin is a portable EXE: put it in a folder you can write to and run it. Settings are stored beside it in mumbin_data. It needs the Microsoft Edge WebView2 runtime, which many Windows PCs already have.

Does Mumbin send my notes to the internet?

No. The app stores notes locally and has no cloud connection and no telemetry. Links you click on purpose open your browser or the matching program. This website is served by GitHub Pages, independently of the app.

How secure is the encryption?

Mumbin derives the key from your password with Argon2id and encrypts with XChaCha20-Poly1305; if someone alters a file, decryption notices. The format is open: you can read it in the source code and in the Python tool.

Who owns my password?

Only you. There is no back door – so nobody but you can open your protected pages, not even the independent tool without your password. Keep it safe and back up your notebook folder.

Will my notes stay readable if Mumbin disappears?

Unprotected pages are plain text files. You open protected pages with the included Python tool and your password – without Mumbin.
